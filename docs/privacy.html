Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Flight Monitor is a personal Android app that shows the status of a flight and sends notifications about changes.

What we collect

Nothing personal. The app does not ask for or store your name, booking reference (PNR), email, phone number, contacts or location. It has no accounts, ads or analytics.

Flight data

The app reads public flight status (times, delays, gates, baggage belt) from our server, which gets it from third-party flight data providers. Only the flight number and date are used for these lookups.

On your device

The last flight status and your notification settings are stored locally on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/mumayank/flight-monitor-releases.
